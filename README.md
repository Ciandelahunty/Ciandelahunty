## About me

I'm currently pursuing an MSc in Financial Mathematics at UCL, following an MA in Economics at the University of Edinburgh and two years as a graduate analyst at HSBC. I'm interested in quantitative research, systematic trading, and macro investing.

## Research interests

- **Market microstructure and price efficiency:** Order books, Liquidity and Transaction costs
- **Systematic investing:** Portfolio Construction, Rebalancing and Backtesting
- **Statistics and econometrics:** Time series, Forecasting and Survival analysis

## **Modules at UCL Include:** Forecasting, Interest Rates and Credit Modelling, Market Risk and Portfolio Theory, Stochastic Processes, Applied Computational Finance, Mathematics and Statistics of Algorithmic Trading

## Featured Projects and Experience

- **Prediction Market Arbitrage Study**: a Python bot that recorded 637,760 order-book snapshots across 186 Polymarket markets over 14.8 days. A pre-registered test of whether outcome prices that should sum to $1 offered profitable trades after fees, order-book depth and capital lock-up. Mispricings appeared 1.2% of the time, but 97% vanished before anyone traded against them.
- **ICAIF 2026 Trading Agent Competition**: an automated agent managing a $1m portfolio of 30 US stocks, rebalancing hourly. Compared rebalancing rules in a backtest on about 263,000 hourly price bars, and chose one that cut turnover tenfold with no loss of Sharpe ratio.

- **HSBC, Graduate Analyst (2024–2026):** rebuilt an interest-rate pricing calculator in SQL, which was rolled out to the sales team for client pricing, and built a survival-analysis (Kaplan–Meier) model of client attrition used in the team's annual financial plan.
